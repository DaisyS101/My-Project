Puppy care questions

A few helpful tips for keeping your puppy happy, healthy, and ready to play.

Puppies have small stomachs and lots of growing to do. Most do well with three or four small meals a day; follow your vet's advice and the feeding guide for their food.

Keep playtime short and gentle, with plenty of naps in between. Your puppy's needs depend on their age and breed, so ask your vet for an exercise plan that's right for them.

Start with simple, positive lessons as soon as your puppy comes home. Reward good behavior, keep sessions brief, and make sure everyone in the household uses the same cues.
